# Client messages

Before writing, reviewing or sending any message to a client, a client's team or a client group, read `${NADAVAI_HOME:-$HOME/nadavai}/plugins/nadavai/skills/client-messages/SKILL.md` and follow it. It keeps every client message in one style (one goal, verified facts, exact links and media, a clear "what I need from you") and one process: draft, approve, send, read back. Before a send, show Nadav anything that could trip him up (promises, money, legal, unverified items) and send nothing like that without his explicit approval. After a send, give him a summary he can rely on without reading the message, and log it locally. A send still needs Nadav's explicit request for that message (`whatsapp.md`).
