# Client messages

Before writing, reviewing or sending any message to a client, a client's team or a client group,
read `${NADAVAI_HOME:-$HOME/nadavai}/plugins/nadavai/skills/client-messages/SKILL.md` and follow it.
This keeps every client message in one style: one goal, verified facts, exact links and media,
a clear "what I need from you", and the draft, approve, send, read-back process. Before every
send, show Nadav a heads-up of anything that could trip him up (promises, money, legal, unverified
items) and send nothing like that without his explicit approval; after every send, give him a
summary he can rely on without reading the message, and log it locally. Sending still needs
Nadav's explicit request for that message (`whatsapp.md`).
