# Virtual phones (an app without a phone)

When a task needs to run, see or operate an Android or iPhone app (a store shell, a Capacitor app, a check of a screen on a phone image), load `nadavai:virtual-phones` (fallback: `${NADAVAI_HOME:-$HOME/nadavai}/plugins/nadavai/skills/virtual-phones/SKILL.md`). The skill holds the tools, the commands and what each platform cannot do. On a machine with no Android SDK or no x86sim user, say what is missing and stop; x86sim is not installed by an agent. The lines below hold whether or not the skill is loaded.

- Android runs on the SDK emulator and is driven with `agent-device`. Maestro stays only for flows a repo already keeps as YAML (a store recording, a CI replay). Another tool is proposed only with a comparison on the same flow.
- An emulator is heavy to start and then stays up: wait for room with `heavy-slot -- true`, start it outside `heavy-slot` on your own AVD and port, and stop it by its own serial as soon as the work ends. Never drive, reconfigure or stop an emulator another session started, never take over a device the tool reports as held by another session, and never run two control tools on one emulator.
- The iPhone simulator (x86sim, on the LENOVO only, under its own Linux user) is one device for the whole machine: one session uses it at a time, under the lock the skill describes. It is never run as Nadav's user or with root.
- No form of a live app is sent to its server from a phone image, and no password is typed into one: reading the screen as text prints a password field's content. A signed-in screen is therefore not available here until a path exists in which the password does not pass through the agent. To press a form's button without sending it, the Android emulator goes offline first and the skill's three checks all show offline; the iPhone simulator has no offline switch, so the button is not pressed there.
- A Codemagic build is asked every time (`acting-on-behalf.md`, money): its minutes are shared with the store builds.
- A check on the iPhone simulator shows what its screenshots show. It never proves the store build, push, Face ID, location, the camera or offline behaviour, and a report of such a check says so. No check on a virtual phone replaces what `store-apps.md` asks of a real device: the app installed from the public listing, and the device recording before a submission.
