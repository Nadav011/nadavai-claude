# my-method: is the way we work right, or doubled and wasted

Nadav has many sessions working without a break on demos, specs, plans and review rounds. He asks whether all of it serves the product itself: what is right, what happens twice, what need not happen, and what should happen at another time. He asked it in six sessions on 2026-11-14 and each answered for its own corner; the question is the same everywhere. How much to do is already his answer, given that day to the sessions that asked him what to cut (in his words, as he typed them: «אני תמיד רוצה הכי מלא שיש אבלא כפוליות או דברים שסתם מעכביםפ אותנו»): the fullest there is, without doubles and without things that only hold the work up, with everything closed in full, in the right and real way and not for show. So nothing he asked for is dropped here: each thing is closed in full, once, in the place where it stays.

1. Measure before you answer, and say where each number came from. For this session's work, or for what he named: how much of it is in the product itself (commits on the app's main branch, units of the plan that are built and checked, screens that work) and how much is around it (rounds of a demo, a spec, a plan or a work order; reviews; drafts; handoff files; tools not yet used on the real thing). `token-audit 1` shows what the last day cost and who spent it, over every session on this machine: say that it is not this project's cost alone.
2. Sort every stream of work that is running or planned into four, each with its number:
   - It carries into the product, and how: a decision of his, a rule that becomes a test, an acceptance line, design values, a finding about real behaviour.
   - It happens twice: the same thing built, checked, measured or written in two places: a demo whose code is thrown away and the app after it, two tools for one measurement, a session that repeats the one under it, two sessions on one file. Look across the sessions before you sort, not only inside this one: ListAgents (this account profile's sessions), the sessions under the other profiles (`/nadavai:my-other` says where each profile lists them), the plan folders and handoffs this work reads, and the method files other sessions already wrote (`~/.omc/handoffs/*method*`, `~/.omc/handoffs/*way-of-working*`). When none of that could be read, say that the answer covers this session only.
   - It need not happen: review rounds beyond what his rule asks for (`working-style.md`, Second model, "Review rounds converge", as that bullet stands when you read it; it is his rule and the test is there, not here), polish of something he has not seen yet, detail that will be written again anyway.
   - It belongs at another time: the full spec of a piece that is not the next to be built (`working-style.md`, Requests: a spec is written right before its piece is built), a check that belongs on the built unit and not on the paper, polish before he approved the direction.
