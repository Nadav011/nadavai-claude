# my-run: carry it to the end without stopping

Nadav is leaving, often for the night, and wants this work finished when he is back.

1. Turn the goal into a short list of what done means here. Each line is something that can be checked: a command that passes, a page that loads, a status that is green. Cover everything his words include. `working-style.md` (Finishing) has the rules for this list. "מקצה לקצה" is his word for it (`~/nadavai/guides/glossary.md`): everything the work really needs is finished, in every link of its chain and at every stage, with nothing left, and first of all what he meant was understood. So the list opens with one line that says what he meant, and then names every link this work has. Which links those are depends on the work; they usually run from his request to the thing working for the person who uses it: built, checked, in main, live on the domain and tried there the way a user would, the store apps and the other computer where it reaches them, other sessions told, and what was learned saved the way `/nadavai:my-save` saves it (a change to a rule or a skill in `~/nadavai` is shown to him first, not made). Leave out only a link this work does not have. A boundary in his words (stop after a named unit, build but do not ship yet, nothing in production until the end) is part of the list: the run ends there.
2. Goal mode is what keeps a session from stopping before the list is closed, and only he can turn it on. Give the list the heading "מה נחשב גמור", in the reply and in the plan file: the ready goal line of his Ctrl+P list points at a list of that name. Say in the first line of the reply that goal mode is not on yet and how he turns it on: Ctrl+P (or Ctrl+Shift+P), the row "לנעול את הסשן על היעד", Enter. If the menu is not installed on this machine (`~/.claude/skills/my-menu` is missing), or he says the row is not there, print one ready `/goal` line for him instead, ending the way Finishing says a goal condition ends. Do not wait for it: start working. Until then this session works through the list, so write it to the plan file at once, where a compaction cannot drop it.
3. Work through the list. Run independent units in parallel, within `model-routing.md` and `machine-load.md`. Check each unit when it is finished, not all of them at the end. Quality comes before speed here: no check is dropped to finish sooner. A parallel agent builds and proves its unit and returns it; the merge, a migration and the deploy are taken by this session, or by a step of a Workflow it runs, when a procedure in the project's files gives that step the action and Nadav approved that procedure (`working-style.md`, Approval gates).
4. What waits for him and what runs without asking is set in `working-style.md` (Approval gates), in `acting-on-behalf.md` and in the project's own files. Read the Approval gates section now instead of working from a remembered list, because it changes. An engineering action follows `~/nadavai/guides/production-change.md` and goes in the "בוצע בלי לשאול" report. A blocked item does not stop the others.
5. Keep a decision trail as you go, because he will ask what was decided without him: append one line per decision to `decisions-made-alone.md` beside the plan: what you chose, what you rejected and why, and the evidence. A decision is a judgment call, a change of scope or a risk you accepted, not a routine step; when no alternative was weighed, write "none considered". When something forces you away from the plan, take the option that is easier to undo, write it in the trail, and continue. A choice that Approval gates or `working-style.md` (Requests) leaves to him, such as a product decision that changes what a person does, sees or pays and that nobody decided, is not made here: it waits for him like a blocked item, and the work that does not depend on it goes on. Write each entry when the decision is made, not at the end.
6. Save the state of the work to its plan and task files as you go, so that a compaction or a restart loses nothing.
7. When the list is met, or only blocked items remain, write the report he will read when he is back: what is done with its evidence and the link to what he can look at, what is blocked and on whom, what you decided alone (from the trail, with its path), and the exact question for each thing that waits on him.
