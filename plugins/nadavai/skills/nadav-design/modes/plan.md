# nadav-design · mode `plan`

## Mode `plan <feature>` (before any code, one feature)

Part of the nadav-design skill. Before this file read the Hard rules of `SKILL.md`, then `shared/rules.md`
and `shared/platform.md`. Every path here is relative to the skill folder, one level above this file.


0. **Is the problem clear?** Declare the tempo and the track. If the problem, audience or whether to build at all is unclear: `/intent:strategize` and `/intent:investigate` (ember) first; if user research already exists: `synthesize-research` (nadavai) before the PRD; if Nadav is about to interview users: the `mom-test` skill (nadavai, from wondelai) shapes the questions. Otherwise say "problem clear" and continue. Evidence: the brief or the one line.
1. **PRD.** `/write-spec` (bundled in the nadavai plugin) for the feature; answer its questions from the repo and PRODUCT.md, ask Nadav only what cannot be inferred. Save to `docs/specs/<feature>.md`. The spec does not wait for his approval: its open product decisions reach him in one batch (AskUserQuestion, up to four per call) while the rest goes on (`rules/working-style.md`, Requests). Evidence: the file.
2. **Job.** `jobs-to-be-done` (nadavai, from wondelai): one job statement per user type, saved into the same spec under "Jobs". Evidence: the section.
3. **Flows and IA.** Intent plugin (wren): `/intent:journey` for the end-to-end flow, `/intent:organize` for IA and navigation, `/intent:wireframe` for the screen structure, `/intent:localize` for the RTL and Hebrew notes. Output as Mermaid in `docs/specs/<feature>.md` under "Flows": happy path plus two failure paths, every decision node has an exit. Evidence: the Mermaid renders.
3b. **The menu with people** (Nadav asked for this step on 13.11.2026). An owner step: it needs people who use the app, so an agent prepares the material and writes down what happened, and never answers in their place. **When:** the feature adds, moves or renames menu destinations in a large app: more than 20 screens reachable from the menu, or more than 7 items at its first level, bar and «עוד» together (this skill's threshold, not his). Otherwise say "small menu" and skip. **What:** first an open card sort to find the groups: one card per topic or screen, 15 to 30, in the users' words ("תזכורות לתור", "קבלות", "שעות פעילות"); each person sorts them into piles and names each pile. Then a tree test of the proposed menu: the menu as plain text, no design, and 5 to 8 tasks from the spec's "Jobs" ("איפה משנים כמה זמן לפני התור יוצאת התזכורת?"); each person says where they would tap, level by level. **With whom:** 5 to 8 people for the card sort and 8 to 10 for the tree test (enough to see the main groups and the labels that fail, read as a direction, not as statistics), from the people who will use the menu (the clinic's reception, the salon's clients), never Nadav, his team or an agent. **How:** on paper (cards and a printed tree) or a free online tool; anything that costs money is asked of Nadav first. **Recorded** in `docs/specs/<feature>-nav-test.md`: the cards, each person's piles and names, the tree, and per task and person whether they reached the right place, whether they went straight there, and their first tap, with what they said in their words. **What changes the menu:** a task that fewer than 7 of 10 people reached, or whose first taps mostly went to another branch, gets a new label or place and is tested again on that part; a group most people made that the menu does not have is put to Nadav as a question. Skipped with "no people yet", said in the spec. Evidence: the file and the menu change it led to, or the skip line.
4. **Heuristics pre-check.** `ux-heuristics` skill (nadavai, from wondelai) on the planned flow, reading `references/cultural-ux.md` for the RTL section. If the feature is a funnel (landing, signup, checkout, onboarding): the `conversion-optimization` skill (nadavai, from wondelai) on the planned flow as well. Then `/intent:evaluate` (vigil) on the wireframes: Nielsen, anti-patterns, WCAG 2.2, and `/intent:fortify` for edge cases, empty and error states, offline. Fold findings into the spec. Evidence: the findings list.
5. **Mockup.** If Nadav wants to see it before code: in an app, no page of its own for one feature (`shared/rules.md`, «An app is built once»): this mode shows the step 3 wireframes, and the feature's screens are first built on invented data in the app's preview by their `screen` runs, where he plays them once the preview's check script passes. In an app that has no preview yet, ask him: build the preview first (setup step 10b, run on his request, isolated as that step says), or, only for a whole flow and within the one-round limit, a direction page of that flow. On a track with no app behind it (`site`, `client-demo` without an app): a low-fi clickable HTML page of the key screen, built from `redesign/templates/section.html` and shown to him as a local file or a claude.ai artifact. Evidence: the page path or link, the wireframes, or "skipped by Nadav".
6. **Hand off.** `/intent:specify` (rune): per-screen spec with behaviour, states, copy and accessibility, saved under "Screens" in the same file; the second model on the spec (`rules/working-style.md`, "Second model"), and fold what survives; then list the screens to build as `- [ ]` checkboxes under "Screens" in the spec (the `screen` mode ticks them and `next` reads them), each becomes a `/ux-screen <name>` run. A plan Nadav asked for is listed and not built until he says to build (`rules/working-style.md`, Finishing); when `plan` runs as a step of a build task he gave, the screens are built in the flow's order, first screen first, and the report says so.
