# my-top: bring it to the highest level

"ברמה הכי גבוהה" is Nadav's bar: the result should stand beside the best of its kind, with nothing generic, half-done or placeholder in it. This command turns that bar into lines that can be checked, and closes the gaps.

What to raise: $ARGUMENTS

If that is empty, it is what this session built or is building.

1. Name the two or three leaders for this kind of thing (a product, a site, a document of this type), open them now, and give the URL of each. Say in concrete terms what makes them the leaders: what a person sees, how fast, how complete.
2. Write the bar for this piece of work as five to ten lines that can each be checked ("לכל מסך יש מצב ריק, מצב טעינה ומצב שגיאה", "אין טקסט מומצא שלקוח היה מזהה"). For a screen, take the lines from the experience standard (`~/nadavai/plugins/nadavai/skills/nadav-design/standard/experience.md`) and check them in a browser, in RTL and at phone width.
3. Hold the work against each line and mark it: at the bar (with the evidence), below it (what exactly is missing), or not checked.
4. Close the gaps that are inside the task he gave, the largest first. Keep the existing design and change what the gap forces. A gap whose fix changes what a person does, sees or pays, and that nobody decided, is his (`working-style.md`, Requests): ask it, and go on with the rest.
5. Show before and after for each gap you closed: a link, a screenshot, a number.
6. Reply in everyday Hebrew (`working-style.md`): the bar; the table against it; what was raised; what is still below and why; and which part an independent reviewer would call the weakest.

Say plainly when something is already at the bar. Raising the level is not adding features he did not ask for.
